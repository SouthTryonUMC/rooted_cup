Read this before touching any file. It exists so future sessions do not drift.

**Source of authority.** `rooted-guiding-document.md` (held by Darryl, not in
this repo) is the source. The design brief v2.2 (Aug 2026) is its tailored

## What this is

rooted. is the neighborhood work of South Tryon Community Missional United
Methodist Church, 2516 South Tryon Street, Charlotte NC 28203. Right now the
shape that work takes is a coffee roastery. The roaster is an expression of
the mission, not the mission itself. Coffee is the classroom, not the point.

What rooted is right now: a paid twelve-month training cohort of four
neighbors, part-time, mixed on purpose; a part-time lead roaster; a 1kg
electric roaster in the church building; sales by subscription, by the bag,
and to institutional accounts; a weekly cohort circle that is not therapy
and not Bible study.

**What rooted is not yet:** a café, a grocery store, a national brand. Older
documents describe a grocery store; they are superseded. Do not design or
write toward grocery. The site must not imply a scale we do not have.

Brand direction: **Cup + Ember** (locked; see "The kit" below).

## Café-first pivot (Sept 2026)

The business plan changed: **a café opens first, roasting comes later.**
The café opens Winter 2026 at 2516 South Tryon Street, pouring a partner
roaster's coffee; neighbors are hired and trained as baristas, paid hourly
from the first day of training. Roasting goes into the same building once
renovation is done; the aim is 2027. Renovation has NOT started and we do
NOT roast yet — no copy anywhere may claim present-tense roasting. The
phases (03's rows) are the café, then the roastery, then wholesale and
subscriptions, and each phase has to carry payroll before the next one
starts. The roast-log design system stays exactly as it is; "Coffee,
roasted here" returns as the 03 heading when the roaster fires (kept in an
HTML comment). Where brief v2.2 assumes roasting today, this pivot wins.

## The claim about the coffee

**The only claim we make about the coffee is that it is good.** No sourcing
story, no origin ethics, no farmer-payment language, no certification badges,
no promised transparency report or any other recurring published document.
If the coffee is good, that is the claim, and it is enough.

This reverses the older kit rule ("we buy from importers who publish what the
farmer was paid, and we print it on the bag"). That line and the bag's FARM
PRICE node were **scrubbed from every file in this repo in Aug 2026, per the
brief. Do not reintroduce them.** The roast log survives without it: origin,
process, roast date, cupped by.

## On incarceration

Some of the people rooted employs carry a record. That is named plainly,
once, in the same words everywhere, and it is never the frame the whole
enterprise hangs on. The cohort is mixed on purpose. Nobody's job title is
their conviction. Design and copy that make incarceration the headline get
it wrong in the other direction. The mission statement's clause is the
canonical naming; the landing page's sentence ("Many of the people we hire
are coming home from incarceration and picking up a trade they can take
anywhere") is the site's version of it.

**Two hiring facts stay deliberately apart** so they never fuse into one
claim about the neighborhoods: (a) most of the people we hire live in
Brookhill Village or Southside — landing stage 01; (b) many are coming home
from incarceration; roaster is the job title — landing stage 03. Do not
merge them into adjacent sentences.

## Hard constraints (from the guiding document, not style)

1. Never describe neighbors as a problem to be solved.
2. Never split helpers from helped. No layout, photo, copy line, or impact
   graphic that separates the served from the serving.
3. Belonging is not probationary. Nothing implies a person earns their place
   by producing or loses it by relapsing.
4. No overclaiming, and no claim we would have to maintain in public.
   The coffee is good — that is the whole product claim (see above).
5. Never take money that requires us to say the work is something it isn't;
   applies to copy written for a funder as much as to the grant.
6. Dignity without skill is condescension. Everything must signal actual craft.
7. Incarceration is named plainly and never made the frame (see above).
8. The church is named, not hidden and not leading. rooted comes from STCUMC
   and Trinity's Table — a fact stated clearly, no church logo lockup in the
   header.
9. The enterprise does not drift from the table it came from. Whatever gets
   built should still make sense in a room where a shared meal is served
   twice a week.
10. Never grow faster than we can hold people.

## Open decisions. Do not settle by default, do not print as claims.

1. Legal structure (LLC, nonprofit, cooperative, two entities). No legal name
   in the footer or on labels until decided. Leave a blank, not a placeholder.
2. What "neighborhood-owned" means mechanically. Until answered it is a
   value, not a fact. **Never use the phrase as a claim.** The approved line
   is: "the people who live here help run it."
3. rooted's formal relationship to STCUMC, and who can change the vision.
4. What we count. **No impact numbers anywhere on the site.** No "X people
   hired," no percentages, no impact band.
5. **Weekly hours and the wage floor.** No hours figure and no wage figure
   appears anywhere — copy, bag, or graphic — until both are settled.
   "Part-time" and "hourly, from the first day of training" are as specific
   as the brand gets.
6. Vented vs ventless roaster (determines the room, signage, any window into
   the roasting space).

## Voice

Two registers at once, never a commercial version and a faith version. A
workforce funder and a Sunday School class read the same page and both find
it addressed to them. Short sentences. Concrete nouns. "We." Spoken cadence:
read every line out loud, and if it sounds like a brochure, cut it. No
epigram closing a paragraph. No three-item lists used for rhythm. Go easy on
em-dashes. Explain the business by how the money moves and who does the
work, not by who it helps.

**Use:** cohort, colleague, craft, kinship, belonging, wage, trade, neighbor,
these blocks, table, roast, harvest, ground, roaster, anchor account, the log.

**Refuse, including in alt text, captions, and commentary:** beneficiary,
at-risk, underserved, giving back, hand up, second chance (as a slogan),
transformation story, ex-con, offender, the less fortunate, helping those in
need, neighborhood-owned, direct trade, cooperative (as plan or denial), any
sourcing or farmer-payment story, any impact number, any hours or wage figure.

## Two failure modes, equally fatal

- **The charity look:** muted earth tones, hopeful sans-serif, hand-drawn
  sprout, clasped hands, percentage-of-proceeds badge. It tells cohort
  members their work is not real.
- **The generated look:** gradients, glassmorphism, emoji icons, three
  feature cards with line icons, drop shadows, 12px radius on everything,
  centered hero over a gradient, copy that sounds like a model wrote it.

If the site could belong to any nonprofit in any city, start over. The
target is a specialty coffee brand with real conviction underneath it —
think roaster, not campaign. The bag has to sit next to Enderly, Not Just,
Undercurrent, and Hex without apology.

## The kit (Cup + Ember, locked Aug 2026)

The built kit satisfies the brief's pass one: direction chosen, question
closed. It keeps the two ideas the brief names as worth keeping — the
taproot as structure, and green as the smallest, most meaningful color.

Source of truth: `rooted-tokens.css` (v1.1). Paste it unchanged. Summary:

- **Palette:** Paper #F5EFE4 (surface, ~58%), Paper-2 #EDE4D5, Roast #221A16
  (text; dark surface, ~30%), Roast-2 #2A201B, Bark #5A4A40 (secondary text
  on light), Chaff #C9B8A2 (secondary text on dark), Green #4F7A3B (period
  and nodes only, under 2%), Ember #E8A33C (see below). Hairlines: #DCCFBB
  on light, #4A3C33 on dark.
- **Green is never text, never a button, never a background.** It passes 3.0
  as a graphic on every surface except Chaff; it fails 4.5 as text
  everywhere. Focus ring is 2px Green (Roast on the Ember surface).
- **Ember** is a full surface carrying Roast text (7.93), or an accent on the
  dark surfaces (7.93 on Roast). NEVER text or graphic on Paper (1.88),
  never under Green (2.33), never carries a node.
- **Type:** Fraunces (display; opsz follows size, SOFT 100, WONK only in the
  wordmark), Instrument Sans (body), Courier Prime (labels, uppercase
  0.06em; the log, sentence case). All OFL 1.1.
- **Scale:** h1 56/40 · h2 36/30 · h3 26/22 · h4 18/17 (Instrument Sans 600)
  · body 17/16 · small 15 · label 12 · log 13. Measure 62ch.
- **Spacing:** 4px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128.
- **Grid:** 12 col to 1120px, 24px gutter and margin; 4 col mobile.
- **Corners and borders:** If it is not a node, it is not round. Radius 0
  everywhere. 1px hairlines. No drop shadows; depth is a surface change or a
  hairline.
- **The taproot:** a vertical rule with nodes, used only where content is an
  ordered sequence (the roast log, the revenue order, the twelve months, the
  landing rail). Never decoration, never a background pattern, never branching.

### Photography

Hands, green coffee, the drum, chaff, the building, South Tryon light. Faces
only with explicit revocable written consent. Never before-and-after. A
cohort member is photographed the way a skilled worker is: at work,
mid-task, competent, not looking at the camera for sympathy. No clasped
hands, no stock portrait standing in for a person, no one photographed to
look poor. Hands with no identifiable face are the stock limit.

## Build constraints

- Static. Netlify (deploys from `main` on SouthTryonUMC/rooted_cup). No
  build step, no framework, no npm. **Flat file structure, no folders.**
- One file per page. Mobile first. Fast. No icon packs, no analytics beyond
  what Darryl asks for. Inline SVG for marks.
- Semantic HTML, headings in order, alt text on every image, visible focus
  states, keyboard-navigable, AA contrast throughout.
- Fonts self-hosted: `rooted-fonts.css` + the `font-*.woff2` files in the
  root (Fraunces variable 300–700 all axes, Instrument Sans variable,
  Courier Prime; latin + latin-ext; SIL OFL 1.1). Works fully offline. Do
  not switch pages to a Google Fonts link tag.

## The site (v2, one landing page — the site of record)

The site is **index.html alone**: a single landing page structured as the
six stages of a roast. The brief's six-page sitemap says "adjust if you have
a better argument"; the landing is that adjustment, approved by Darryl. It
links `rooted-fonts.css`, `rooted-tokens.css`, `rooted-landing.css`,
`rooted-landing.js`.

### The stage flow (the page's spine)

00 Green (hero, Paper) → 01 Drying (what rooted is, Paper-2) → 02 First
crack (how we work, **Ember**) → 03 Development (the enterprise, Roast-2) →
04 Drop (the longer plan, Roast) → 05 Cupping (the conversation + form,
Roast) → footer (Roast). **The page darkens the way a roast does — that arc
is the design.**

### The rail (the taproot, reinterpreted)

The fixed left rail (≥1040px) is the taproot as a roast gauge: Paper surface
always (so Green nodes always pass), spine + six nodes, active node at full
node-size, Fraunces temperature readout in °C (21/160/196/207/212/—). On
mobile it is the bottom-left chip. aria-hidden (decorative duplicate of the
section tags).

### Copy decisions (Darryl, Aug 2026)

- **No lot, no blend, no tasting notes named anywhere.**
- The vision line opens 01 as its display statement; it appears once.
- The vision and sign-off stagewords end in a green period (`.gp`) — the
  kit's period, passing as graphic on Paper-2 (3.99) and Roast (3.41).
  Never on Ember. The 00/01 seam carries a hairline so the Paper to
  Paper-2 step registers on phones.
- The mission is the 02 pull quote (`.mission` in rooted-landing.css):
  display size between body and stageword, one emphasis rule, no box.
- The lede is the 01 paragraph; the hiring naming now lives in the mission.
- No "neighborhood-led / coalition-built," no cooperative sentence, no
  "a ministry of" — the church line is the fixed sentence, in 04 and the
  footer.
- Confirmed facts: Homeboy Industries lineage; Deep Time at Trinity UMC,
  Asheville, coffee social enterprise since 2023; equipment specified,
  compliance pathway mapped, raising toward launch.
- The sign-off appears as the 05 stageword + footer "Stay rooted."
- Reference points for calibration, not copying: Deep Time (Asheville),
  Enderly (Charlotte), Homeboy Industries (LA), Haywood Street Congregation
  (Asheville; the Welcome Table methodology behind Trinity's Table).

### The form (Netlify)

One form, `name="rooted-site"`: name, organization, email, interest select
(coffee / wholesale / funding / partnership / neighborhood / press / other),
message. Honeypot `bot-field`, AJAX submit with inline confirmation in
rooted-landing.js; with JS off, Netlify's default success page. **Netlify
form detection must be enabled and the site redeployed once**, or the form
collects nothing. Off-Netlify the fetch fails and the inline message says to
email — expected, not a bug. Lay out so a real cart can drop in later.

### Photos

**None on the landing, by decision.** Type and the surfaces carry the page.
HTML comments mark the slots (hero strip, 02, 04, 05) for real photographs
later, under the photography rules above.
