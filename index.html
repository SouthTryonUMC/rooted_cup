Roasted on South Tryon.

rooted is a neighborhood enterprise in South Charlotte. We roast coffee, we hire and train neighbors to do the work, and the people who live here help run it. Most of the people we hire have been through the justice system. We hire them as roasters, and roaster is the job title.

The roasting room and the fellowship hall are the same room.

How the work works

How this coffee sells

We are building the business in a particular order. Steady accounts come first, because payroll has to be reliable before anything else can be.

Anchor accounts

Standing wholesale orders from churches, schools, and offices. Contracted, invoiced, and predictable enough to plan payroll around.

Open an account

Subscriptions

Recurring bags shipped to households. Better margin, and it builds a base of people with a stake in how this turns out.

Say you want one

Pop-ups

Markets, events, and being present in the neighborhood. It doesn’t move much volume, but it’s how people meet us and how new hires learn the counter.
