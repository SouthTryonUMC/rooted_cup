The orange. Coffee at first crack. One splash per piece, used one of two ways.

Do
Ember on RoastRoast on Ember

A full background with Roast text on it, like this band. Or a small accent on a dark background: a link, a button.

Never

Ember text or shapes on Paper, which is too faint to read. Green on Ember. Ember as a second accent beside another color.
