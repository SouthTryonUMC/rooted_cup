How to use the brand · one page · v1.0 · Aug 2026

The mark

  • Always lowercase. Always with the period. Never "Rooted", never "ROOTED", never "rooted" without the dot.
  • The period is green. In one-color printing the period matches the letters.
  • Leave space around it on every side equal to the height of the o.
  • Smallest size: 24px on screen, 8mm in print. Smaller than that, use the small mark (the line with the green dot).
  • Never stretch, squash, rotate, outline, add a shadow, or put it inside a shape.
  • Use the files. Do not retype it in another font.

  • Green is never a word, a button, or a background. If you are typing in green, stop.
  • No other colors. No gradients.

Type

  • Fraunces for headlines. Instrument Sans for everything you read. Courier Prime for labels and the roast log.
  • All three are free at fonts.google.com. If you do not have them, use Georgia, Arial, and Courier New and send the file to Darryl before it prints.
  • Corners are square. No rounded boxes, no drop shadows.

Words

  • Say: we, neighbors, cohort, roaster, craft, wage, trade, table, these blocks.
  • Never say: beneficiary, at-risk, underserved, giving back, hand up, second chance, transformation story, ex-con, offender, the less fortunate, helping those in need.
  • Never claim: neighborhood-owned, direct trade, any sourcing or farmer-pay story, any number of people served or lives changed. The coffee is good. That is the whole claim.
  • Short sentences. Say what the business does and who does the work. Do not explain who it helps.

Lines that are fixed. Copy them exactly.

Good work. Good coffee. Good news.

rooted is a neighborhood enterprise in South Charlotte. We roast coffee, we hire and train neighbors to do the work, and the people who live here help run it. Most of the people we hire have been through the justice system. We hire them as roasters, and roaster is the job title.

rooted grew out of South Tryon Community United Methodist Church and the neighbors of Brookhill Village and Southside.

Let's grow something beautiful together. Stay rooted.

Photos

  • People at work, mid-task, competent. Hands, beans, the drum, the room, the building.
  • Faces only with written permission the person can take back. No before-and-after, ever.
  • No clasped hands, no stock photos, no one photographed to look poor, no photos of the housing without the people who live there.
  • Daylight, no filters, square corners.

If you are not sure

  • Would this look right on a bag next to Enderly? Would a cohort member want it on a hoodie? Would a funder read it without asking us to soften it? If any answer is no, send it to Darryl before it goes out.
