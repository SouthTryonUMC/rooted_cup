Support

What support looks like.

rooted runs on coffee revenue, grants, and gifts. This page covers all three: the accounts that grow the first, and the giving that carries the rest while it grows.

Buy it standing

The most durable support is a standing order. An anchor account from a church, a school, or an office is coffee money that becomes wage money, on a schedule we can plan payroll around. It costs your budget what coffee already costs it.

Open an account

Give

Year one, the coffee pays part of the wages. Not all of them. Gifts close that gap on purpose, so the wage never waits on the market.

While the legal structure is being decided, gifts move through South Tryon Community UMC. Write to darryl@rootedclt.org and we will tell you exactly how a gift lands and what it funds. We would rather answer that question person to person than print a form that gets ahead of the paperwork.

Partner congregations

Pour rooted on Sunday. A coffee-hour account is small to a church budget and large to a payroll. If your congregation wants more than an order, come see the room, bring your youth group on a roast day, or put us in front of your missions committee. We will bring the coffee.

rooted grew out of a congregation, so we know what Sunday coffee usually tastes like. Part of the pitch is fixing that.

For funders and partners
