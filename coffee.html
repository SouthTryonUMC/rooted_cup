What the bag tells you

The front of the bag is the roast log. Origin, process, roast date, and who cupped it, in the order it happened. The back carries what the farmer was paid for this lot, from the importer’s published sheet. The information a buyer wants and the information a funder wants are the same information, printed in the same place.

Where the coffee comes from

We buy from importers who publish what the farmer was paid, and we print it on the bag.

We don’t use sourcing language we can’t verify. A published price is a fact. It sits on the back of the bag next to our own honest numbers, and you can check it against the importer’s sheet yourself.

Subscriptions

Recurring bags, monthly or every other week, starting after launch. Saying you want one now is not a commitment. It tells us how much coffee to plan the first roasts around.

Noted, and thank you.

You’re on the waitlist with a subscription flag on your name. When subscriptions open, you choose the cadence then.

Wholesale and institutional accounts

Standing orders from churches, schools, offices, and cafés are what we plan payroll around. We deliver on and around South Tryon and ship beyond it. Tell us what you pour now and roughly how much, and we’ll bring samples when the roaster is hot.

We’ll be in touch.

Expect an email from darryl@rootedclt.org within a few days. If the roaster is hot by then, it comes with a sample schedule.
